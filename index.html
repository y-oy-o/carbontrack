Simple Tips to Lower Your Carbon Footprint

  • Walk, bike, or use public transit for short trips.
  • Eat one meat-free meal per day.
  • Switch to LED bulbs and unplug idle devices.
  • Lower your thermostat a few degrees in winter.
  • Choose renewable energy plans if available.
